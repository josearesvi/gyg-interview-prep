# Interviewer, wrap-up questions (reported by candidates, 2026)

Answer these out loud in ≤ 90 seconds each. Frameworks are in `docs/MOCK_INTERVIEW.md`.
1. "How do you use AI in your day-to-day work? Give a concrete example."
2. "Which tasks should engineers **not** use AI for, or be very careful with?"
3. "A junior colleague ships AI-generated code they don't fully understand. How do you mentor them?"
4. "What did you leave unfixed today, and in what order would you do it?"
